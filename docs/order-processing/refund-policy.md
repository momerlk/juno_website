# Juno Refund Policy

Status: customer-facing draft — Pakistani legal and tax approval required before publication
Version: 1.1, 30 July 2026

This policy explains how Juno processes an approved refund. It does not take away any customer right given by Pakistani law.

## 1. When a refund may be approved

A refund may follow an eligible return, seller cancellation, unavailable item, duplicate/extra payment, failed order, lost parcel, agreed partial refund, or another right under the law or sale terms.

Refund approval and refund payment are different events. Juno will confirm when the payment instruction has actually been issued.

## 2. Refund amount

The approved amount may include:

- price paid for the affected item/quantity;
- shipping paid where the whole order failed or the law/policy requires it;
- taxes or charges attributable to the refunded supply;
- any agreed lawful partial adjustment.

For a customer-choice partial return, the original outbound shipping charge may be non-refundable if it was clearly disclosed and the delivery service was completed. No deduction is made for a seller/Juno error except as permitted by law.

Juno will show the calculation before closing the case.

## 3. Refund route

- Prepaid orders: refund to the original payment method where technically and legally possible.
- COD orders: refund to a verified Pakistani bank account or supported mobile wallet supplied through Juno's secure process.
- Store credit: only with the customer's informed agreement; it is not forced in place of a monetary refund where money is due.

Juno never asks for a password, OTP, PIN, or full card credentials to make a refund.

## 4. Timing

Juno aims to issue an approved refund within 7 business days after the required return inspection or, where no return is required, after approval. The bank, wallet, or payment provider may take additional time to display the funds. The case will show the payment reference/date once issued.

If payment fails because the beneficiary details are invalid, support will request corrected details through the secure process and the target period restarts after verification.

## 5. Cancellations

An order cancelled before dispatch is refunded after payment/booking reversal checks. A cancellation requested after carrier handover may be handled as a delivery refusal/return and may wait for carrier confirmation and parcel recovery. Mandatory rights and seller/Juno fault remedies remain unaffected.

## 6. Partial orders and discounts

For a partial refund, Juno uses the discounts, charges and prices saved when the order was placed. Juno does not recalculate an old order using today's prices.

## 7. Tax and invoice records

Juno does not delete or alter the original invoice. An approved refund creates the applicable credit note, revised digital invoice reference, and accounting entries. These documents identify the supplier responsible for the affected item.

## 8. Questions or disputes

Contact Juno with the order/case number and payment reference if the stated banking period has passed. Juno will investigate the payment trail and provide the next update time. This policy does not restrict access to a court, consumer authority, regulator, or another remedy available by law.
