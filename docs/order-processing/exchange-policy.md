# Juno Exchange Policy

Status: customer-facing draft — Pakistani legal approval required before publication
Version: 1.1, 30 July 2026

This policy applies when an eligible Juno item is exchanged for another size, color, version, or replacement. It does not take away any customer right given by Pakistani law.

## 1. Request period

Request an exchange within 7 calendar days after delivery. For a damaged, defective, wrong, counterfeit, incomplete, or misdescribed item, report it as soon as reasonably possible, preferably within 48 hours, without limiting a mandatory legal right.

## 2. Eligibility

An exchange may be approved when:

- the seller delivered the wrong item/variant or a defective item;
- the product page permits size/color exchange and replacement stock exists;
- another replacement remedy is agreed or required by law.

For a customer-choice exchange, the item must normally be unused, unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and complete with tags, seals, accessories, gifts, and packaging. The change-of-mind exclusions in the [Return Policy](return-policy.md) also apply.

## 3. Stock and price

An exchange is subject to replacement stock. Juno will not substitute without the customer's approval.

- Same product/price: no item-price difference.
- Higher-priced approved replacement: customer pays the clearly disclosed difference through an approved method before dispatch.
- Lower-priced approved replacement: Juno refunds the difference under the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).

Promotions are applied according to the original transaction and exchange terms; completed orders are not repriced at today's promotional rate unless Juno expressly agrees.

## 4. Logistics cost

The responsible seller/Juno pays reasonable collection and redelivery costs for a confirmed wrong, defective, fake, incomplete, or badly described item. For a customer-choice size/color exchange, the customer may need to pay the collection/redelivery charge shown before approval.

## 5. Process

1. Contact Juno with the order number, item/quantity, requested replacement, reason, and reasonable evidence.
2. Juno confirms eligibility, stock, price difference, and shipping cost.
3. Use only the authorized return label/instructions.
4. Juno/seller inspects the returned item when required.
5. Juno creates a separately tracked replacement shipment; the original tracking number is never reused.
6. Juno sends the new tracking reference and applicable invoice/credit note.

An unboxing video can assist but is not mandatory for every claim and its absence alone does not automatically reject a genuine defect/wrong-item claim.

## 6. If exchange cannot be completed

If approved replacement stock becomes unavailable or the exchange cannot reasonably be completed, the customer may choose an eligible alternative or refund, subject to law and the disclosed price difference.

## 7. Repeat exchanges

Customer-choice exchange is normally limited to one exchange per item unless Juno agrees otherwise. This limit does not restrict remedies for a replacement that is itself wrong, defective, counterfeit, unsafe, incomplete, or misdescribed.
