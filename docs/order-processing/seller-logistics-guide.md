# Juno Seller Logistics Guide

Status: current seller-portal workflow, 31 July 2026

## 1. Open the seller order

Go to **Seller Dashboard → Orders** and open the Juno seller order. Use the portal order, not a screenshot or chat message, to check the product, variant, quantity and delivery details.

## 2. Check and pack the item

Before packing, verify the item is correct, clean, undamaged, complete and matches the selected variant and quantity. Use strong, clean packaging and remove old labels. Do not substitute an item or change customer delivery details outside Juno.

If stock is missing, an item is damaged, or the order details look wrong, contact Juno immediately and do not mark the order packed.

## 3. Upload the required packing evidence

While the order is **Confirmed**:

1. Upload one JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo for each order item in its row.
2. Upload one JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo of the sealed parcel with its airway bill attached.
3. Wait for each file to show **Saved privately**.
4. Select **Mark packed** only after every item photo and the parcel photo are saved.

The portal blocks packing until this evidence is complete. Do not send private customer information or unrelated documents in the parcel photos.

## 4. DEX booking and handover

Juno staff prepare the official manual DEX workbook and record the DEX consignment number in the admin portal. The carrier is DEX; sellers do not select a carrier or create a separate DEX booking.

When the airway bill and handover instructions are available, attach the correct label to the sealed parcel. Keep it ready for the agreed DEX handover, ensure the courier receives the correct parcel, and retain any handover proof. Tell Juno promptly if the label is wrong or the parcel cannot be handed over.

## 5. Delivery problems and returns

Tell Juno immediately about a failed handover, damage, loss, return, or customer complaint. Do not arrange an off-platform replacement, address change, second attempt, or separate courier shipment. Keep returned or disputed stock separate and provide factual evidence requested by Juno.

## 6. Seller payment

Delivery is not seller payment. Juno must receive the matched DEX payment, create the seller statement, transfer the amount, and record payment proof. Check **Seller Dashboard → Statements** for statement status and report a discrepancy with the exact order number or amount.
