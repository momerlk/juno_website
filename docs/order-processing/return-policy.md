# Juno Return Policy

Status: customer-facing draft — Pakistani legal approval required before publication
Version: 1.1, 30 July 2026

This policy applies to eligible products ordered through Juno. It does not take away any customer right given by Pakistani law. The seller shown on the order/invoice remains responsible for the product; Juno manages the return process.

## 1. Request period

Contact Juno within 7 calendar days after delivery to request a return. For an item that is damaged, defective, incomplete, counterfeit, or different from the order, please report it as soon as reasonably possible, preferably within 48 hours, so that we can preserve courier and seller evidence. Missing the preferred 48-hour period does not by itself remove a mandatory legal right.

## 2. Eligible reasons

A return may be approved when:

- the item is damaged, defective, fake, very different from its description, or unsafe;
- the wrong item, size, color, quantity, or variant was delivered;
- an advertised component or accessory is missing;
- the item is eligible for change-of-mind return under its product page and remains in the required condition;
- another remedy is required by applicable law.

## 3. Condition for change-of-mind returns

Unless the item was defective, wrong, or misdescribed, it must be unused, unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and returned with original tags, seals, accessories, manuals, gifts, and packaging in resalable condition. Reasonable handling needed to inspect the item is allowed where applicable.

## 4. Normally excluded from change-of-mind returns

For hygiene, safety, customization, or deterioration reasons, the following are normally not returnable merely because the customer changed their mind:

- underwear, intimate apparel, opened cosmetics/personal-care products, and pierced jewelry;
- made-to-order, personalized, altered, or custom-sized products;
- perishable or time-sensitive products;
- opened sealed goods that cannot safely be resold;
- gift cards, vouchers, or digital products after supply;
- clearance/final-sale items where the exclusion was clearly disclosed before purchase.

These exclusions do not apply when the product is defective, wrong, counterfeit, unsafe, materially misdescribed, or where the law requires a remedy.

## 5. How to request

Use Juno support and provide the order number, affected item/quantity, reason, and reasonable evidence. Photos of the product, packaging, courier label, defect, and serial number may help. An unboxing video may help but is not a mandatory condition for every claim and its absence alone does not automatically invalidate a genuine claim.

Juno will provide a case number and instructions. Do not send an item to the seller or another address without authorization; an unauthorized parcel may be delayed or lost.

## 6. Collection and shipping cost

For a confirmed wrong, damaged, defective, fake, incomplete, or badly described item, Juno/the responsible seller arranges or repays reasonable return shipping according to the seller agreement and law.

For an approved change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for the disclosed return cost. Juno will state the amount before authorization.

## 7. Inspection and outcome

After receipt, Juno or the seller records the condition of the item and packaging and checks it within the promised time. The result may be a refund, exchange/replacement, suitable repair, another solution agreed with the customer, or a rejection that clearly explains why.

If a change-of-mind item is materially used, incomplete, altered, or damaged after delivery, Juno may reject the return or apply a lawful, disclosed reduction. Defect claims are assessed on their evidence and are not rejected merely because packaging was opened to discover the defect.

## 8. Risk and records

Keep your authorized return receipt/tracking number until the case closes. Juno retains the original order/invoice and records any approved adjustment through a credit note or other legally required document.
