# Juno Marketplace Seller Agreement

Status: plain-language contract draft — a Pakistani lawyer and tax adviser must approve it before signing
Version: 2.0, 30 July 2026

This agreement is between:

- **Juno:** [legal name, registration number, NTN and address]; and
- **Brand/Seller:** [legal name, registration/CNIC, NTN, STRN if needed and address].

Start date: [date]

The fee sheet, service deadlines, product rules and [Seller Logistics Guide](seller-logistics-guide.md) are part of this agreement.

## 1. What Juno does

Juno lists the brand's approved products, helps customers place orders, collects or arranges payment, helps with delivery and customer support, deducts agreed fees and pays the brand.

Unless a signed document says otherwise, the brand remains the seller and owner of its products until they are sold to the customer. Juno is the marketplace and payment collection agent. Juno does not buy the brand's stock or promise a certain number of sales.

## 2. Information the brand must provide

The brand must provide correct and current:

- legal/business name and owner information;
- NTN, STRN and company details where required;
- bank account in the brand/approved owner's name;
- dispatch address and contacts;
- product, stock and price information.

The brand must tell Juno when something changes. Juno may pause products or payments while important information is missing, unsafe or being checked.

## 3. Products and listings

The brand promises that its products are legal, real, safe, correctly described and do not copy someone else's protected work.

Product pages must show the correct:

- product and material;
- size, color and measurements;
- price and stock;
- condition and origin where important;
- warnings, warranty and return limits.

The brand may not list illegal, fake, unsafe or restricted products. Juno may hide or remove a product that creates a customer, legal or platform risk.

## 4. Prices, commission, DEX charges and tax

The signed fee sheet states:

- brand price;
- Juno commission rate;
- who pays DEX delivery fees, VAT and other charges;
- who pays for returns or failed delivery;
- allowed discounts or promotions;
- payment timing and any agreed hold.

Juno commission is calculated on brand price, not shipping or the higher customer display price.

Juno may not deduct a new type of charge that is not in the agreement/fee sheet or required by law. DEX charges and taxes must be shown clearly on the brand statement.

The brand is responsible for taxes and invoices that legally belong to the brand. Juno is responsible for taxes and invoices that legally belong to Juno's services. Both sides will provide the information needed for FBR reporting and digital invoicing.

## 5. Orders

The brand should only process an order shown in the seller portal. A screenshot or WhatsApp message alone is not enough.

The brand must provide the exact product, size, color and quantity ordered. It must not:

- send a replacement without customer approval recorded by Juno;
- ask the customer for extra money;
- move the order outside Juno;
- contact the customer unless Juno allows it for that case;
- cancel without giving the real reason.

Repeated stock mistakes, wrong products or late orders may lead to a warning, payment hold or account suspension under the fee/deadline sheet.

## 6. Packing and pictures

For every order, the brand must upload:

1. one clear picture of each product before it is packed; and
2. one picture of the closed parcel with the airway bill attached and readable.

The brand must follow the Seller Logistics Guide, use safe packaging and mark **Packed** only when the parcel is fully ready for pickup.

After the brand marks it packed, Juno emails the Juno team and CCs the brand.

The brand is responsible for a loss or reasonable cost caused by its wrong product, unsafe/poor packing, incorrect parcel information or missed handover, where the fee sheet and law allow it.

## 7. DEX booking and delivery

Before Daraz Open Platform integration, Juno staff create the DEX booking and provide the airway bill. After integration, the brand can download the airway bill from the seller portal.

The brand must not create another DEX booking for the same Juno order or change the airway bill by hand.

The brand must make the parcel available on time and keep handover proof. DEX tracking is the main delivery record unless better evidence shows that it is wrong.

## 8. Customer problems, returns and exchanges

Juno normally speaks to the customer. The brand must answer Juno's questions and inspect approved returns within the agreed deadline.

The brand is responsible for the remedy and related agreed costs when it sends a wrong, defective, fake, unsafe, incomplete or badly described product.

The customer policies shown at the time of purchase apply. They cannot remove customer rights that Pakistan law says must be provided.

The brand must not reject a real complaint only because there is no unboxing video.

## 9. When the brand becomes eligible for payment

Delivery alone is not enough. An order becomes eligible for a brand statement after:

- DEX delivered the required products;
- Daraz/DEX paid Juno;
- Juno marked the order **DEX Payment Received**;
- any agreed complaint/return hold has ended;
- there is no open problem blocking payment;
- the brand's bank and tax details are valid.

## 10. Brand statement and payment

The statement shows:

- orders and tracking numbers;
- COD collected and DEX amount received;
- DEX delivery fees, VAT and taxes;
- brand price;
- Juno commission rate and amount;
- approved refunds/corrections;
- final amount to pay the brand.

After payment, the seller portal shows the statement, Juno invoice, bank payment proof, amount, date and reference.

The brand must report a statement mistake within [15] business days and point to the exact order/amount. A clear mistake, fraud or required tax correction may still be fixed later.

Juno may hold only the amount reasonably connected to an open fraud, return, chargeback, legal/tax, bank-verification or seller-breach issue. Where practical, Juno should pay the part that is not disputed.

## 11. Invoices and records

The brand allows Juno to create/send the customer's brand invoice on the brand's behalf only where the law and tax setup allow it. The invoice must name the correct seller.

Juno separately creates its commission/service invoice for the brand. The brand payment statement is a payment explanation, not the same document as a tax invoice.

Old invoices and paid statements are not deleted. Corrections use a linked credit, debit or adjustment document.

## 12. Customer information and security

The brand may use customer information only to prepare and ship the Juno order. It must not use it for marketing, sell it, share it or move the customer off Juno.

Only trained staff should see customer information. The brand must tell Juno immediately, and no later than [24] hours, if information is lost, stolen or shared incorrectly.

## 13. Brand names, pictures and other content

The brand gives Juno permission to use its name, logo, product details and product pictures to list, advertise and support its products while this agreement is active and for reasonable order/legal records afterward.

The brand promises that it owns or has permission to use this content. Juno keeps ownership of the Juno platform, software, name and original content.

## 14. Private business information

Juno and the brand must protect each other's private customer, price, financial, security and business information. They may use it only for this agreement unless the law requires disclosure.

## 15. Checks and legal compliance

The brand must keep product, stock, packing, dispatch, tax, return and complaint records. Juno may ask to check records connected to a serious order problem, payment difference or legal duty.

Both sides must follow Pakistan's consumer, tax, product safety, electronic transaction, competition, anti-bribery and other applicable laws.

## 16. Responsibility for harm

The brand is responsible for losses, claims or penalties caused by its illegal, fake, unsafe, defective or rights-infringing product, tax failure, customer-data misuse or serious breach.

Juno is responsible for losses directly caused by Juno's own platform/service breach. The final contract should include a fair money limit and list serious matters—such as fraud, unsafe goods, unpaid money and data misuse—that cannot use that limit.

## 17. Pausing or ending the agreement

Either side may normally end the agreement with [30] days' written notice.

Juno may pause products, orders or payment immediately for customer safety, fraud, fake/illegal products, missing legal/tax details, a data leak, a government instruction or a serious breach. Juno should explain the reason unless the law prevents it.

A fixable serious breach normally has [10] business days to be corrected after notice.

Ending the agreement does not remove unfinished orders, returns, warranties, tax records, money owed, private-information duties or existing disputes. Juno will prepare a final statement after valid holds are settled.

## 18. Changes to these terms

Juno may improve everyday operating rules with reasonable notice. Changes to commission, payment, important seller costs or legal responsibility require the notice and acceptance method in the signed fee sheet.

Electronic acceptance may be used where Pakistan law allows it. Juno saves the accepted version, account, date and time.

## 19. Pakistan law and disagreements

Pakistan law applies. Managers from both sides should first try to solve a disagreement within [15] business days.

If they cannot, the final signed agreement will use [the agreed courts in Pakistan or a lawyer-approved arbitration clause]. A customer, regulator or either side may still use a legal right that cannot be removed by contract.
