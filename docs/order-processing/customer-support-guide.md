# Juno Customer Support Guide

Status: current admin-portal workflow, 31 July 2026

## Use the correct seller order

Use **Admin → Orders** as the source of truth. A multi-brand checkout can create separate seller orders and tracking numbers. Before sharing or changing information, match two details already on the order, such as order number, phone number, email, city, or product.

Never request an OTP, password, PIN, card information, or banking password.

## Address questions and corrections

1. Open the seller order and use **Address review → Create ChatGPT prompt**.
2. Copy the message, contact the customer through the approved channel, and ask only for the missing address facts.
3. In the portal, update the customer/delivery details when required and enter the reviewed result in **Formatted address**.
4. Save the review. Use **Customer confirmed address** only after the customer approves the final address.

The AI prompt helps staff write a clear question; it does not verify the address.

## Order and delivery questions

Read the product, quantity, address, total, status and saved DEX tracking information directly from the order. Explain only what is shown:

- **Confirmed** means the seller order is confirmed.
- **Packed** means the seller uploaded the required item and sealed-parcel evidence and marked it packed.
- A saved **DEX tracking/consignment number** and tracking URL are the booking details available to share.
- **Delivered** means the order status says delivered.

Do not promise a delivery date, pickup, address change after booking, cancellation, COD change, or a new delivery attempt unless operations confirms it.

## When a seller needs help

Ask the seller to use **Seller Dashboard → Orders → the order**. They must upload one photo per item and one sealed-parcel photo with airway bill before **Mark packed** becomes available. They should report stock, label, parcel, or pickup issues to Juno immediately rather than making a separate shipment.

## Financial and privacy boundaries

Support must not tell a seller that payment is due merely because an order was delivered. Juno first records the DEX payment, creates a seller statement, transfers the money, and records the payment reference and private proof. Sellers can see their statements in **Seller Dashboard → Statements**.

Use customer information only for the order. Never share another customer’s details, a private proof file, or a permanent signed upload URL.

## Escalate immediately

Escalate to operations when there is suspected fraud, threats, unsafe/fake goods, a privacy incident, a lost parcel, a dispute over a marked delivery, or a bank/payment issue. Record the order number, facts, current owner and next update time in the appropriate internal workflow.
